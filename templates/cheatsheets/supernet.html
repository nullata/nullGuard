Supernet

Common default ranges that this app adds or removes when Full Tunnel is toggled on a client.

Same subnet behavior

Clients default to routing only the server's VPN subnet.

Example:

leave blank
One VPN range

Full Tunnel adds a broader VPN range so clients can reach related VPN subnets.

Example:

10.240.0.0/16
All private 10.x networks

Full Tunnel routes every 10.x private address through the VPN. Use only if that is intentional.

Example:

10.0.0.0/8
All IPv4 traffic

Full Tunnel becomes a true IPv4 internet tunnel.

Example:

0.0.0.0/0

Avoid broad ranges that overlap users' home or office LANs unless you want that traffic captured by the VPN.
