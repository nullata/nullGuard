Allowed IPs

Common ways to choose what traffic this client sends through the tunnel.

VPN only

The client only sends traffic for the WireGuard VPN subnet through the tunnel. Normal internet traffic stays local.

Example:

10.240.0.0/24
Full tunnel, IPv4

The client sends all IPv4 traffic through the VPN, including internet traffic.

Example:

0.0.0.0/0
Full tunnel, IPv4 and IPv6

The client sends all IPv4 and IPv6 traffic through the VPN.

Example:

0.0.0.0/0, ::/0
VPN plus one office LAN

The client can reach VPN peers and one private network behind the server or another routed peer.

Example:

10.240.0.0/24, 192.168.50.0/24
Single host route

The client only routes one specific private machine through the VPN instead of the whole LAN.

Example:

10.240.0.0/24, 192.168.50.25/32

More details: WireGuard overview
